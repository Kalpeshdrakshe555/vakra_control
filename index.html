Frequently Asked Questions

Does the hardware work without Internet?
Yes, our app has an "Offline Mode". The sensors and deterrents run on local edge logic. However, for remote alerts to your phone, a basic 4G connection is required.
Is there a warranty on the hardware?
Yes, all our hardware components come with a 1-year replacement warranty. The solar panels have a 5-year performance guarantee.
Who will install the setup?
AgriGuard 360 is designed for DIY plug-and-play installation. However, we also provide a network of local technicians in supported districts who can install it for a nominal fee.
