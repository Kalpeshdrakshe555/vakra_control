# 3D Printing Vendor Product Submission Form
## Field Documentation & Purpose

### Document Purpose

This document explains every field collected through the **3D Printing Product Submission — Vendor Onboarding** form.

The purpose is to help the internal team understand:
- What information we collect from vendors.
- Why each field is required.
- How the information will be used for product listing, pricing, customization, order fulfillment, quality control, and vendor management.

---

# 1. Vendor Details

## Vendor / Brand Name
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies the vendor or brand responsible for manufacturing the product. It is also useful for internal vendor records and product attribution.

## Contact Person
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies the primary person responsible for communication regarding products, orders, production issues, and other operational matters.

## WhatsApp / Mobile Number
**Why we collect it:**  
Used for quick operational communication, especially for order confirmation, production updates, urgent issues, and capacity-related communication.

## Email Address
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides a formal communication channel for vendor records, documentation, agreements, and important operational communication.

## City
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps identify the vendor's general operating location and can be useful for logistics and operational planning.

## District
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides additional location information for internal vendor management and logistics planning.

## State
**Why we collect it:**  
Confirms the vendor's state location and supports regional vendor management.

## Dispatch / Pickup Location
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies the actual location from where the finished product will be collected or dispatched. This is important for shipping and fulfillment planning.

---

# 2. Product Details

## Product Name
**Why we collect it:**  
The primary name used to identify the product internally and later create its Shopify product listing.

## Product Category
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps organize products into a structured catalogue and makes future navigation, search, and collection management easier.

## Product Description
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides the raw product information required to create an accurate customer-facing product description.

## Product Use / Purpose
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps the team understand what the product is intended for and communicate its purpose clearly to customers.

## Product Photos
**Why we collect it:**  
Required for evaluating the product and creating the visual content for the online product listing.

## Product Video
**Why we collect it:**  
Optional visual material that can help demonstrate the product's appearance, functionality, size, or usage.

## Printing Technology
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies how the product is manufactured, such as FDM/FFF or Resin. This helps the team understand production characteristics and material limitations.

## Material
**Why we collect it:**  
Material affects product properties, appearance, durability, use cases, and customer expectations. It is also important product information for the listing.

## Available Colours
**Why we collect it:**  
Defines the actual colour choices that customers can select during the initial limited customization model.

## Product Dimensions
**Why we collect it:**  
Allows customers to understand the physical size of the product and helps the team create accurate product specifications.

## Approximate Product Weight
**Why we collect it:**  
Useful for product information and internal shipping/fulfillment calculations.

## Ready Stock / Made-to-Order
**Why we collect it:**  
Determines whether the vendor already has inventory or needs to manufacture the product after receiving an order.

## Normal Production Time
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps us estimate realistic order fulfillment time and communicate expected delivery timelines to customers.

## Assembly Required
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies whether the customer will receive a ready-to-use product or whether assembly will be required.

## Product Limitations / Customer Instructions
**Why we collect it:**  
Captures important limitations such as heat sensitivity, handling requirements, fragility, or other information customers should know before purchasing.

---

# 3. Customization

## Available Customization Options
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies the simple customization options the vendor can actually provide during the pilot, such as colour, size, or minor changes.

## Exact Customization Choices
**Why we collect it:**  
We need the exact available choices so that only realistic options are displayed to customers.

## Additional Customization Cost
**Why we collect it:**  
Determines whether a specific customization requires an additional charge and helps calculate the final product price.

## Additional Production Time
**Why we collect it:**  
Some customization may increase manufacturing time. This information helps set accurate fulfillment expectations.

---

# 4. Pricing

## Base Vendor Price per Unit
**Why we collect it:**  
Defines the amount the vendor expects to receive for supplying one standard unit. The platform can use this to determine the final customer price and platform margin.

## Additional Customization Cost
**Why we collect it:**  
Captures extra vendor charges for supported customization so they can be included when calculating the final customer price.

## Minimum Order Quantity
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies whether the vendor requires a minimum quantity for fulfilling a product.

## What Is Included in Vendor Price
**Why we collect it:**  
Clarifies whether printing, finishing, assembly, packaging, or other services are already included in the quoted price.

## Other Product Charges
**Why we collect it:**  
Prevents unexpected costs by identifying any additional charges that may apply to the product.

## Price Validity
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps the team know how long the submitted vendor price can be considered valid before reconfirmation is required.

## Pricing Notes
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides space for any pricing conditions or information that does not fit into the structured fields.

---

# 5. Production Capacity

## Normal Units Produced Per Day
**Why we collect it:**  
Shows the vendor's normal daily production capability for the product.

## Normal Units Produced Per Week
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides a broader view of the vendor's production capacity and helps with demand planning.

## Maximum Simultaneous Orders
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies how many orders the vendor can handle at the same time without creating fulfillment problems.

## Maximum Units During High Demand
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps us understand how much additional production the vendor can handle during periods of increased demand.

## Normal Manufacturing Time After Order
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides the expected production time after an order is received and helps calculate fulfillment timelines.

## Urgent Orders
**Why we collect it:**  
Determines whether the vendor can handle urgent production requests and what capacity is available for them.

## Raw Material Availability
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps identify whether the vendor normally has the required material available or may face frequent production delays.

## Frequently Unavailable Materials / Colours
**Why we collect it:**  
Allows the team to avoid promising customization options that may frequently become unavailable.

## Ability to Pause Orders
**Why we collect it:**  
Allows the vendor to temporarily stop accepting orders when production capacity is full, reducing the risk of delayed orders.

---

# 6. Quality

## Product Inspection Before Dispatch
**Why we collect it:**  
Confirms whether the vendor checks the product before shipping and helps establish a basic quality-control process.

## Quality Checks Performed
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps the team understand what the vendor checks before an order leaves their facility.

## Normal Printing Imperfections
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies expected characteristics such as minor layer lines or surface marks so the team can distinguish normal appearance from actual defects.

## Product Fragility
**Why we collect it:**  
Determines whether additional packaging or special handling may be required during shipping.

## Material Limitations
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps prevent customers from using the product in conditions for which its material is not suitable.

## Consistent Reproduction
**Why we collect it:**  
Confirms whether the vendor can reproduce the same product consistently across multiple orders.

## Defective Product Replacement
**Why we collect it:**  
Defines whether the vendor can replace products affected by manufacturing or printing defects.

## Replacement Turnaround Time
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps the team estimate how quickly a defective order can be resolved for the customer.

---

# 7. Design Rights / Licensing

## Design Ownership / Type
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies whether the product is an original design, licensed design, commercially licensed model, or another type of design.

## Commercial Manufacturing & Selling Rights
**Why we collect it:**  
Confirms that the vendor has the necessary rights or permission to manufacture and commercially sell the product.

## Commercial License / Rights Proof
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides an indication that the vendor can demonstrate commercial rights when the design is licensed or purchased.

## Third-Party Logos / Characters / Trademarks / Artwork
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies potential intellectual-property concerns before the product is listed on our platform.

## Vendor Rights Declaration
**Why we collect it:**  
Creates a clear vendor confirmation that they have the necessary rights or permissions and that the submitted product information is accurate.

---

# 8. Packaging & Shipping

## Packaging Responsibility
**Why we collect it:**  
Defines who is responsible for preparing the product for shipment.

## Packaging Type
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps us understand how the product is normally protected during transportation.

## Approximate Packed Weight
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides the estimated weight of the actual shipment package for logistics and shipping calculations.

## Package Dimensions
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps estimate shipping requirements and determine whether special handling may be needed.

## Special / Fragile Packaging
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies products that require additional protection during transportation.

## India-Wide Shipping
**Why we collect it:**  
Confirms whether the vendor can fulfill orders across India or has geographical shipping limitations.

## Normal Dispatch Time
**Why we collect it:**  
Defines how quickly the vendor can hand over a completed order for shipping after production.

## Tracking Number Availability
**Why we collect it:**  
Allows the platform to provide shipment tracking information to customers when available.

## Shipping / Handling Limitations
**Why we collect it:**  
Captures any special restrictions or conditions that could affect fulfillment.

---

# 9. Additional Product Information

## Why Customers Would Want This Product
**Why we collect it:**  
Helps the team understand the product's customer value and create better marketing/listing content.

## What Makes the Product Different or Useful
**Why we collect it:**  
Identifies the product's key selling points and differentiators.

## Important Customer Information
**Why we collect it:**  
Captures any information customers should know before purchasing that may not have been covered elsewhere.

## Additional Notes
**Why we collect it:**  
Provides a flexible field for useful information that does not fit into the predefined questions.

# Internal Data Flow

The information collected through this form will primarily support the following workflow:

Vendor
→ Product Submission
→ Internal Product Review
→ Product Approval
→ Shopify Listing
→ Customer Order
→ Vendor Production
→ Quality Check
→ Dispatch
→ Customer Delivery

---

# Pilot-Phase Principle

During the initial 3-month testing period, the objective is to keep the catalogue and operational workflow simple.

We should collect enough information to:

- Create accurate product listings.
- Understand what each vendor can actually produce.
- Offer only realistic customization.
- Set appropriate pricing.
- Avoid accepting orders beyond vendor capacity.
- Estimate production and dispatch time.
- Maintain basic quality standards.
- Verify product/design rights.
- Handle fulfillment efficiently.

We should avoid collecting information that does not directly support these objectives.

The form can be expanded later based on real problems, vendor feedback, customer behaviour, and operational data collected during the pilot.
